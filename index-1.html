Who can work with Kingdom Spirit Technologies?+

We welcome enquiries from individuals, entrepreneurs, startups, small businesses and established organisations. We will discuss your needs and confirm whether our services are a good fit.

Can I request a service that is not listed?+

Yes. Tell us about the problem you are trying to solve. We can review the request and let you know whether we can help or recommend a suitable next step.

How much do your services cost?+

Pricing depends on the scope, features, integrations and requirements of each project. Contact us with a brief description and we will discuss the appropriate options.

Do you work with clients remotely?+

Many digital projects can be planned and delivered remotely. We will agree on communication, milestones and handover arrangements before work begins.

What happens after I make an enquiry?+

We will review the information you provide, discuss the project with you and outline the next steps, including scope and any relevant quotation.
